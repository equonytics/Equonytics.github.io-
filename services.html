Fractional Leadership

Senior data leadership, without the full‑time hire.

You need someone who can set the data strategy, talk to the board, and build the team. But you may not need them five days a week. We step in as your fractional CDO or VP of Analytics—on a schedule that fits your budget and growth stage.

Strategy & Roadmap

Set the direction

We work with your exec team to define the analytics vision, pick the right initiatives, and make sure every data effort ties to a business goal.

  • Data strategy development
  • Roadmap & investment planning
  • Board‑level communication
Team & Capability

Build the muscle

Mentor your analysts, help with hiring, and create a culture where people actually use data to make decisions.

  • Talent assessment & hiring
  • Analytics team mentoring
  • Upskilling & training programs
Governance & Vendor

Keep vendors honest

Oversee tool selection, negotiate contracts, and ensure every purchase serves the business. We don't concern ourselves with the vendor’s sales targets.

  • Vendor evaluation & management
  • Data governance framework
  • Cost optimization

Dashboarding & Reporting

Turn raw data into decisions at a glance

We design and build interactive dashboards and automated reports that put the right information in front of the right people at the right time.

Dashboard Design

Beautiful, functional, intuitive

We create dashboards that are visually clear, easy to navigate, and aligned with your KPIs.

  • Tableau, Power BI, or custom
  • KPI selection and hierarchy
  • Mobile‑responsive layouts
Automated Reporting

Reports that send themselves

We set up scheduled, self‑refreshing reports so your team spends less time compiling and more time acting.

  • Email and Slack delivery
  • Drill‑down and alerts
  • Subscription management
Custom & Tool‑Agnostic

Not locked into one platform

When Tableau, Power BI, or Looker isn’t the right fit—or you don’t want the license cost—we build fully custom dashboards on a Python backend, with the same clarity and none of the vendor lock‑in.

  • Python data pipelines & APIs
  • Any BI tool, or fully custom
  • You own the code, not us
