On-time Delivery & Margins

Finding the service level that actually pays for itself

A regression on 21,316 orders that told the client where to stop chasing on-time delivery — and where to start.

View analysis
On-time delivery vs. gross margin lifthover to inspect
Every 1-point improvement was associated with a 0.4-point increase in gross margin, but only after service crossed 92%.
Change made: the client stopped funding uniform service-level targets network-wide and redirected the budget to push underperforming lanes past 92%, where the margin actually shows up.

Reading the regression

A metric only earns its keep if it has the capacity to change your strategy.

Below 92%

Fix the floor

Stop spreading effort evenly.

Above 92%

Harvest the premium

Put capital into lanes above the line.

The marginal-ROI trap

Do not apply the 0.4 multiplier across the whole network.
